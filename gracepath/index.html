Support

GracePath is a calm companion for Scripture, prayer and reflection — Bible reading with narration, daily and traditional prayers, a private journal and a quiet digital sanctuary.

Contact: pericprew@gmail.com
Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem. We usually reply within two business days.

What is free?

The Bible with narration, the daily verse, daily and traditional prayers, prayer reminders, the journal and one sanctuary space are free forever.

What does GracePath Pro include?

All sanctuary spaces, the complete soundscape library, advanced reading plans, deeper devotional statistics, more personalisation and journal export. Pro is US$3.99 per month or US$29.99 per year (local prices are shown in the app). Eligible new subscribers can try Pro free for 7 days.

How do I cancel?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → GracePath → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before a trial or billing period ends to avoid being charged. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Pro is missing on my new iPhone

Open the Pro page in the app and tap “Restore purchases” while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data stored?

Your journal, prayers, highlights and reading progress are stored on your iPhone. No account is needed. See the Privacy Policy.

Narration sounds robotic

GracePath uses the voices built into iOS. For a more natural voice, download an Enhanced or Premium voice in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices, then choose it in GracePath → My Faith → Settings → Narration voice.

I don't receive prayer reminders

Check that notifications for GracePath are allowed in iOS Settings → Notifications, and that Focus modes are not silencing them.

支援

GracePath 是陪伴你讀經、禱告與默想的安靜空間：有朗讀的聖經、每日與傳統禱文、私人日記，以及寧靜的數位聖所。

回報問題時，請附上 iPhone 型號、iOS 版本與發生問題前的操作步驟。我們通常會在兩個工作天內回覆。

免費版有什麼？

聖經與朗讀、每日經文、每日與傳統禱文、禱告提醒、日記，以及一個聖所空間，永久免費。

GracePath Pro 包含什麼？

所有聖所空間、完整環境音庫、進階讀經計畫、更深入的靈修統計、更多個人化選項與日記匯出。Pro 每月 US$3.99 或每年 US$29.99（App 內會顯示當地價格）。符合資格的新訂閱者可免費試用 7 天。

如何取消訂閱？

iPhone「設定」→ 點你的名字 →「訂閱項目」→ GracePath →「取消訂閱」。請在試用期或計費週期結束前至少 24 小時取消，以免被收費。刪除 App 不會取消訂閱。

換新 iPhone 後 Pro 不見了？

在 App 的 Pro 頁面點「恢復購買」，並使用同一個 Apple ID 登入即可。

我的資料存在哪裡？

日記、禱告、畫線與讀經進度都儲存在你的 iPhone 上，不需要註冊帳號。詳見隱私權政策。

朗讀聲音聽起來很機械？

GracePath 使用 iOS 內建語音。若想要更自然的聲音，請到 iOS「設定 → 輔助使用 → 朗讀內容 → 聲音」下載「增強版」或「高品質」語音，再到 GracePath「我的信仰 → 設定 → 朗讀聲音」選擇。

沒有收到禱告提醒？

請確認 iOS「設定 → 通知」已允許 GracePath，並檢查專注模式是否將通知靜音。
