Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

GracePath is a personal space for Scripture, prayer and reflection. We built it so that what you record stays yours. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

In this policy, “GracePath”, “we” and “us” mean the publisher of the GracePath app, and “you” means the person using it.

In short

• GracePath works without an account. Your journal, prayers, reading progress and other records are stored on your device.

• There are no ads. We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use third-party advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

• An account is optional and is only needed if you choose to sync your records between devices.

• Your private journal is never uploaded unless you turn on “Include private journal in sync”.

• Face ID is handled entirely by your device. Biometric data never leaves your device and never reaches us.

Information stored on your device

The following is stored in the app's private storage on your device and is not sent to us:

• Journal entries — devotions, SOAP studies, testimonies, gratitude, prayer cards and meditations.

• Prayer history, favourite prayers and prayers you write yourself.

• Bible reading progress, highlights, bookmarks, favourites and notes.

• Candles and intentions from the Sanctuary, devotional sessions and counters, and reading-plan progress.

• Your settings, such as language, tradition, Bible translation, reading preferences and reminder times.

Bible texts, sounds and artwork are included in the app, so reading, listening and praying do not require an internet connection.

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, your device may include the app's local data in those backups, under Apple's terms and your own settings.

Optional account and cloud sync

If you create an account, we use Supabase, a cloud database provider, for sign-in and sync. To sign in you enter your email address and we send you a one-time code. We do not ask for a password.

When you are signed in and use sync (a GracePath Pro feature), copies of these records are stored in your account so they can be restored on your other devices: reading progress, highlights, bookmarks, notes, prayer history, favourite and personal prayers, candles and intentions, devotional sessions, counters and plan progress.

Your private journal is excluded from sync by default. It is uploaded only if you switch on “Include private journal in sync” in Account & sync. Turning this off stops further uploads; entries already uploaded remain in your account until you delete your cloud data.

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access rules on the database are designed so that each account can read and change only its own records.

We use your email address only to sign you in and, when necessary, to contact you about your account. We do not send marketing emails without your consent.

Subscriptions and purchases

GracePath Pro is sold as an auto-renewable subscription through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your card or payment details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active and to restore purchases. RevenueCat receives purchase information from Apple — such as the product, purchase and expiry dates and transaction identifiers — linked to a random app user ID. If you sign in to an account, its identifier is linked to your purchases so that Pro can follow you to your other devices.

Apple and RevenueCat handle this information as described in their own privacy policies.

Notifications

Prayer reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent through a server, and we do not collect push-notification tokens. You can change or turn off reminders in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

Face ID and app lock

If you turn on app lock, GracePath asks iOS to verify you with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Verification happens entirely on your device, and the app receives only a success or failure result. Your biometric data never leaves your device and is never available to GracePath or to us.

Narration and device permissions

Scripture and prayer narration uses the speech voices built into your device. GracePath does not use your microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location.

Where it is offered, you can choose an optional cloud voice. Only the text being read aloud — Scripture, prayers or guided meditations, never your journal — is then sent over an encrypted connection to our speech server, which uses Microsoft Azure Speech to create the audio. The audio is cached so the same text is not sent again. The built-in voices remain the default and work offline.

What we never do

• We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

• We do not show advertising or share data with advertisers or data brokers.

• We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other companies' apps and websites.

• We do not access the contents of your journal, prayers or notes, unless you ask us for help with your account and give permission, or the law requires it.

Service providers and legal bases

We share information only with the providers needed for the features you choose: Apple (App Store purchases), RevenueCat (subscription status) and Supabase (optional account and sync). They process data on our behalf under their own terms and may store it in countries other than yours, with appropriate safeguards.

To tell you when a new version is available, the app asks Apple's public App Store lookup service for the latest version number. This request contains no personal information.

Where data-protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we process account and sync data to provide the service you ask for (performance of a contract) and purchase records to meet legal and accounting obligations. We do not process your data for any other purpose without your consent.

We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the rights and safety of our users. Because most of your data exists only on your device, we usually hold nothing to disclose.

Keeping and deleting your data

• On your device: delete individual journal entries at any time, or remove all of your records in Settings → Erase all local data. Deleting the app also removes its local data from your device.

• In the cloud: Account & sync → Delete cloud data & account permanently deletes the records stored in your account and closes it. Data on your device is kept.

• Cloud data is kept while your account exists. After deletion, copies may remain in our provider's routine backups for a limited time before they are overwritten.

• Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat as required for accounting and legal purposes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority.

You can do most of this directly in the app — edit or delete entries, export your journal, or delete your cloud data. For anything else, contact us using the details below and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Children

GracePath is suitable for a general audience but is not directed at children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Accounts and subscriptions should be created by, or with the permission of, a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections and per-account access rules. Records on your device are protected by iOS data protection, and you can add GracePath's app lock for extra privacy. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please keep your device and your email account protected.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: pericprew@gmail.com

隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 9 日

本文件的翻譯版本如與英文版本有出入，在法律允許的範圍內，以英文版本為準。

GracePath 是屬於你個人的讀經、禱告與默想空間。我們打造它，是為了讓你所記錄的一切始終屬於你。本政策說明本 App 會處理哪些資訊、這些資訊保存在哪裡，以及你擁有哪些選擇。

在本政策中，「GracePath」、「我們」是指 GracePath App 的發行者，「你」是指使用本 App 的人。

摘要

• GracePath 無需帳號即可使用。你的日記、禱告、讀經進度及其他紀錄都儲存在你的裝置上。

• 沒有廣告。我們不會出售或出租你的個人資訊，也不使用第三方廣告、分析或追蹤工具。

• 帳號為選用，僅在你選擇於裝置之間同步紀錄時才需要。

• 除非你開啟「同步時包含私密日記」，否則你的私密日記絕不會上傳。

• Face ID 完全由你的裝置處理。生物辨識資料絕不會離開你的裝置，也絕不會傳送給我們。

儲存在你裝置上的資訊

以下資訊儲存在本 App 於你裝置上的私有儲存空間中，不會傳送給我們：

• 日記：靈修、SOAP 讀經、見證、感恩、禱告卡與默想。

• 禱告紀錄、收藏的禱告，以及你自己撰寫的禱告。

• 讀經進度、螢光標示、書籤、收藏與筆記。

• 聖所中的蠟燭與祈禱意向、靈修時段與計數，以及讀經計畫進度。

• 你的設定，例如語言、傳統、聖經譯本、閱讀偏好與提醒時間。

聖經經文、聲音與美術素材皆內建於本 App 中，因此閱讀、聆聽與禱告都不需要網路連線。

如果你使用 iCloud 備份或電腦備份，你的裝置可能會依照 Apple 的條款及你自己的設定，將本 App 的本機資料納入這些備份中。

選用帳號與雲端同步

如果你建立帳號，我們會使用雲端資料庫服務供應商 Supabase 進行登入與同步。登入時，你需輸入電子郵件地址，我們會寄送一次性驗證碼給你。我們不會要求你提供密碼。

當你登入並使用同步功能（GracePath Pro 功能）時，下列紀錄的副本會儲存在你的帳號中，以便在你的其他裝置上還原：讀經進度、螢光標示、書籤、筆記、禱告紀錄、收藏與個人禱告、蠟燭與祈禱意向、靈修時段、計數與計畫進度。

你的私密日記預設不會同步。只有當你在「帳號與同步」中開啟「同步時包含私密日記」時，日記才會上傳。關閉此選項會停止後續上傳；已上傳的日記會保留在你的帳號中，直到你刪除雲端資料為止。

資料透過加密連線（HTTPS）傳送。資料庫的存取規則經過設計，使每個帳號只能讀取及變更自己的紀錄。

我們僅將你的電子郵件地址用於讓你登入，以及在必要時就你的帳號與你聯繫。未經你同意，我們不會寄送行銷電子郵件。

訂閱與購買

GracePath Pro 以自動續訂訂閱的形式，透過 Apple 的 App Store 販售。付款由 Apple 處理；我們絕不會取得你的信用卡或付款資料。

我們使用 RevenueCat 確認你的訂閱是否有效，並回復購買項目。RevenueCat 會從 Apple 接收購買資訊（例如產品、購買日期與到期日期，以及交易識別碼），並與一組隨機的 App 使用者 ID 連結。如果你登入帳號，該帳號的識別碼會與你的購買項目連結，讓 Pro 能延續到你的其他裝置上。

Apple 與 RevenueCat 會依照其各自的隱私權政策處理這些資訊。

通知

祈禱提醒是在你的裝置上排定的本機通知，不會經由伺服器傳送，我們也不會收集推播通知權杖。你可以隨時在本 App 內或 iOS「設定」中更改或關閉提醒。

Face ID 與 App 鎖定

如果你開啟 App 鎖定，GracePath 會請 iOS 透過 Face ID、Touch ID 或你的裝置密碼驗證你的身分。驗證完全在你的裝置上進行，本 App 只會收到成功或失敗的結果。你的生物辨識資料絕不會離開你的裝置，GracePath 或我們也絕不會取得。

朗讀與裝置權限

經文與禱告的朗讀使用你裝置內建的語音。GracePath 不會使用你的麥克風、相機、聯絡人、照片或位置。

在有提供的地區，你可以選用雲端朗讀聲音。屆時只有正在朗讀的文字（經文、禱文或引導默想，絕不包含你的日記）會透過加密連線傳送到我們的語音伺服器，由 Microsoft Azure 語音服務產生音訊。音訊會被快取，相同的文字不會再次傳送。內建聲音仍是預設選項，且可離線使用。

我們絕不會做的事

• 我們不會出售、出租或交易你的個人資訊。

• 我們不會顯示廣告，也不會與廣告商或資料仲介分享資料。

• 我們不使用第三方分析或追蹤工具，也不會跨其他公司的 App 與網站追蹤你。

• 我們不會存取你的日記、禱告或筆記內容，除非你就帳號問題向我們請求協助並給予許可，或法律有所要求。

服務供應商與法律依據

我們只會與提供你所選功能所需的供應商分享資訊：Apple（App Store 購買）、RevenueCat（訂閱狀態）與 Supabase（選用帳號與同步）。它們依照其各自的條款代表我們處理資料，並可能在採取適當保護措施的前提下，將資料儲存在你所在國家以外的地方。

為了在有新版本時提醒你，本 App 會向 Apple 公開的 App Store 查詢服務取得最新版本號碼。此查詢不包含任何個人資訊。

在適用 GDPR 等資料保護法律的情況下，我們處理帳號與同步資料，是為了提供你所要求的服務（履行契約）；處理購買紀錄，則是為了履行法律與會計義務。未經你同意，我們不會為任何其他目的處理你的資料。

如果法律有所要求，或為了保護使用者的權利與安全，我們可能會揭露資訊。由於你的大部分資料只存在於你的裝置上，我們通常沒有任何可揭露的資料。

資料的保存與刪除

• 在你的裝置上：你可以隨時刪除個別日記，或在「設定 → 清除所有本機資料」中移除你的所有紀錄。刪除本 App 也會從你的裝置上移除其本機資料。

• 在雲端：「帳號與同步 → 刪除雲端資料與帳號」會永久刪除儲存在你帳號中的紀錄並關閉帳號。你裝置上的資料會保留。

• 只要你的帳號存在，雲端資料就會保留。刪除後，副本可能會在我們供應商的例行備份中保留一段有限的時間，之後即被覆寫。

• 購買紀錄會由 Apple 與 RevenueCat 依會計與法律目的之要求保存。

你的權利

依你居住地的不同，你可能有權存取、更正、匯出或刪除你的個人資訊，反對或限制特定的處理行為，並向資料保護主管機關提出申訴。

這些操作大多可以直接在本 App 中完成：編輯或刪除日記、匯出日記，或刪除你的雲端資料。如有其他需求，請透過下方的聯絡方式與我們聯繫，我們會在法律規定的期限內回覆。

兒童

GracePath 適合一般大眾使用，但並非以未滿 13 歲（或你所在國家規定之最低年齡）的兒童為對象。我們不會在知情的情況下收集兒童的個人資訊。帳號與訂閱應由家長或監護人建立，或經其同意後建立。如果你認為有兒童向我們提供了個人資訊，請與我們聯繫，我們將予以刪除。

安全性

我們採取合理的保護措施，包括加密連線與以帳號為單位的存取規則。你裝置上的紀錄受 iOS 資料保護機制保護，你也可以啟用 GracePath 的 App 鎖定，以獲得更多隱私保障。沒有任何儲存或傳輸方式是絕對安全的，因此請妥善保護你的裝置與電子郵件帳號。

本政策的變更

我們可能會隨著本 App 的變更而更新本政策。我們會更新本頁頂端的日期；若有重大變更，也會在本 App 中通知你。
